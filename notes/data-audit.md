## Decisions this audit supports

1. Train on the **87** rows with a real price; keep the 21 priceless rows aside as a demo prediction set.
2. Drop as too sparse: `final size`, `Bathrooms`.
3. Drop as near-constant: `Essential_Utilities_score`, `Premium_features_score`, `Wellness_score`.
4. Log-transform the target.
5. Impute what remains **inside** the CV pipeline, never before splitting.
6. Check `Latitude`/`Longitude` against `Neighborhood` for multicollinearity before keeping both.
